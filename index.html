01 Our Story

A Place to Come Back To

Taverna Tsardaki began in 2019, with a love for Greek hospitality and the pleasure of bringing people together around the table. Today, Chef and owner Kostas Tselekidis keeps that spirit alive in his own way.

Over the years, Tsardaki has grown with Nea Skioni while staying true to what matters most: a welcoming atmosphere, generous hospitality and food made to be shared.

03 The Table

The Way We
Welcome You

This year, Tsardaki begins a new chapter in a new location, right on the town promenade, next to the harbour. The setting has changed, but what matters around the table has not: good food, familiar flavours and the warmth of a traditional Greek taverna.

Come for a long lunch, an easy dinner or simply a table by the harbour. There is room for relaxed afternoons, shared plates and conversations that last a little longer than planned.
